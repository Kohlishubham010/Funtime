Choosing the Right SunMoon Ride for Your Project

Every amusement venue has different operating and entertainment requirements. A SunMoon Ride installed inside a permanent amusement park may require a different project approach compared with an attraction planned for a fair, resort, leisure park, or entertainment centre.

Important factors such as available operating area, expected rider capacity, target audience, ride placement, visitor movement, surrounding attractions, and project layout should be considered during ride planning.

Fun Finity Amusement works with amusement businesses, developers, and project owners to understand their requirements and explore suitable SunMoon Ride solutions according to their space and entertainment concept.
